# Character-list ownership markers

Multi-character Discord lists display `Character Name 💞` when the character is married to anyone in the current server. Ownership in another server and wishes do not qualify. Characters in an escape attempt remain owned until their marriage ends or transfers.

Covered displays:

- Catalog top lists.
- Franchise character lists, with or without ranks.
- Marriage lists, including franchise-grouped lists.
- Trade and gift harem lists.
- Wishlists, including characters no longer in the catalog.

Ownership is checked when a list opens and whenever its page changes. Previously posted lists are not automatically updated after marriages or divorces. Dropdown selectors, individual character cards, and action confirmations are outside this presentation rule.

The marker follows the escaped character name, before any remaining row metadata. It does not replace rank, franchise, or unavailable-catalog information.
